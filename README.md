\# Goal



This research presents a genome-wide association study (GWAS) analysis in order to find the SNPs associated with Alzheimer’s disease (AD). The research was made on the ADNI batch 1 data. More about the ADNI project at https://adni.loni.usc.edu/about/.



\# Conclusion



The research revealed 21 SNP possibly associated with AD. The genetic risk score association model was validated with ROC-AUC.



The annotated genes were examined. Most part of the known genes are related to different diseases of nervous system. Other known genes are also associated with diseases: primary ciliary dyskinesia and tumour.
